MedReflexed is building intelligent visualization and analysis tools for [medical/healthcare domain]. Our MVP focuses on [user workflow — interactive, animation-rich interface]. We're a small, fast-moving team shipping the first version of a product that [impact].

## The Role

You'll be the **first frontend engineer** at MedReflexed, owning the interactive UI and component architecture from day one. This is a high-leverage hire — you'll shape how the product feels and performs.

### What you'll do
- Design and build a scalable component architecture in React (or Vue)
- Own state management (Redux, Jotai, Zustand, Context—your choice)
- Bring clinical/domain data to life with smooth animations and intuitive interactions
- Work closely with our designer on design systems and responsive layouts
- Ship features fast while keeping the codebase maintainable
- Mentor junior engineers as we grow the team

### Your strengths
- **3+ years** building production React or Vue applications
- **Comfortable with animation libraries** (Framer Motion, Lottie, Three.js) and performance optimization
- **Strong state management** experience; you can architect Redux/Jotai/Zustand solutions
- **Ownership mindset**: you see a problem, you fix it; you don't wait for specs
- **Bias toward shipping**: you know the difference between "perfect" and "good enough"
- **Communication**: you can explain technical tradeoffs to designers and non-technical stakeholders

### Nice to have
- Experience with medical/healthcare products or regulated industries
- Familiarity with TypeScript and testing (Vitest, React Testing Library)
- Backend understanding (API design, data flow, performance implications)
